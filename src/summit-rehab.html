About Summit Rehab

We treat the whole route, not just the pain point.

Summit Rehab was built on a simple idea: recovery works best as a plotted route with a clear summit, not a string of disconnected appointments.

01
Assess where you stand
02
Plot the route to your goal
03
Adjust the plan as you climb

Most rehab clinics hand every patient the same set of exercises and hope the calendar does the rest. We start differently — with a real assessment of your strength, movement patterns, and goals, so the plan reflects your body, not a template.

Our kinesiologists work one-on-one with a small caseload, which means your therapist actually remembers your last session when you walk in for your next one. Progress gets measured and the plan gets adjusted, session by session, until you're back to the life you had before the injury — or further.

We also handle the parts of recovery nobody enjoys: direct billing to ICBC, WorkSafeBC, and most extended health providers, so you can focus on getting better instead of chasing paperwork.
